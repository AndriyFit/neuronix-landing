# Neuronix: Google Ads Ecommerce Validation Plan

## Goal

Validate one focused commercial hypothesis before expanding into AI, CRM, automation, OpenCart and other verticals.

**Hypothesis:** Google Ads can consistently generate qualified leads from entrepreneurs/business owners who have a product or existing business and want to launch an online store.

**Initial media budget:** 200 UAH/day (~6,000 UAH/month).

## ICP v1

Primary audience:
- Entrepreneurs with an existing product/business who are not yet selling effectively online.
- Offline retailers moving online.
- Instagram/Facebook sellers who want their own store.
- Small distributors/manufacturers launching ecommerce.
- Businesses accepting orders manually and wanting a proper online storefront.
- New ecommerce founders who need a store launched from scratch.

Not the focus of this test:
- Existing stores seeking deep automation.
- AI-agent-only demand.
- CRM-only demand.
- 1C/integration-only demand.
- OpenCart optimization-only demand.
- Corporate websites.

## Landing strategy

Do not radically replace the existing homepage during the first experiment.

Create a dedicated landing page:

`/uk/ecommerce`

Google Ads traffic for the ecommerce test should point to this page, while the homepage remains the broader Neuronix brand/product page.

## Core positioning

Recommended direction:

> **Запустимо ваш інтернет-магазин під ключ**

Supporting message:

> Від структури та дизайну до готового магазину, підключення оплати, доставки та аналітики. Ви отримуєте магазин, готовий приймати перші замовлення.

Primary CTA:

> **Обговорити запуск магазину**

Secondary CTA:

> **Подивитися приклад магазину**

## Price communication

Keep the existing low entry price as a qualification tool, but explain scope clearly.

Recommended framing:

> **Запуск магазину від $350**
> Підберемо рішення під кількість товарів, функціонал і ваш бюджет.

The final page must explicitly define what is and is not included in the $350 offer. Do not advertise a scope that cannot actually be delivered at that price.

## Landing structure v1

1. Hero: clear ecommerce launch promise, starting price, primary CTA.
2. Deliverables: store, responsive design, catalog, cart/order flow, payment, delivery, analytics, basic SEO setup where applicable.
3. “Вам сюди, якщо…”: Instagram seller, offline store, manual orders, new ecommerce business.
4. Process: discovery → store structure/UX → development → integrations → launch.
5. Proof/case: strongest relevant ecommerce case with screenshots and concrete results.
6. Why Neuronix: launch now, keep the architecture ready for future CRM/AI/automation.
7. Packages/pricing: clear scope and qualification.
8. FAQ: price scope, timing, products, delivery, payment, CRM expansion, maintenance.
9. Final CTA: discuss launch / get estimate.

## Google Ads v1

Use Search campaigns only for the initial validation.

Start with high-intent queries around:
- створення інтернет магазину
- створити інтернет магазин
- розробка інтернет магазину
- розробка інтернет-магазину
- замовити інтернет магазин
- зробити інтернет магазин
- інтернет магазин під ключ
- створення магазину онлайн

Do not mix AI, CRM, generic website development, OpenCart or automation intent into this initial campaign.

Review Search Terms frequently and build negative keyword coverage from real traffic.

## Experiment discipline

Do not test many variables at once.

Suggested sequence:

### Test 1: Offer
A: “Інтернет-магазин під ключ від $350”
B: “Запустимо ваш інтернет-магазин за 7–14 днів”

### Test 2: CTA
A: “Обговорити запуск”
B: “Отримати оцінку магазину”

### Test 3: Hero framing
A: “Запустимо ваш інтернет-магазин під ключ”
B: “Перетворіть свій бізнес на магазин, який продає онлайн 24/7”

Only change one major variable per test cycle.

## Conversion tracking

Measure the full funnel, not only form submissions:

Google Ads click → landing visit → CTA click → form start → form submit → qualified lead → call/meeting → sale.

Primary business KPI:

**Cost per qualified lead**, not raw lead count.

Also track:
- Search term quality.
- Landing conversion rate.
- CTA click-through rate.
- Form completion rate.
- Qualified lead rate.
- Cost per qualified lead.
- Sales rate.
- Customer acquisition cost when enough data exists.

## Lead qualification

The first form should remain low-friction, but capture enough information to distinguish real ecommerce demand.

Suggested fields:
- Name.
- Phone / Telegram / email.
- What do you sell?
- Do you already have a website/store? (Yes/No).
- Approximate number of products (optional).

If the user already has a site, ask for its URL. Do not require an existing URL from people starting from zero.

## Success criteria for the first phase

The first phase is a validation exercise, not a scale exercise.

We want enough data to answer:
1. Which search terms generate qualified ecommerce intent?
2. Does the dedicated ecommerce landing convert that intent?
3. Which offer/CTA framing produces better qualified leads?
4. What is the cost of a qualified lead?
5. What objections/questions appear repeatedly?
6. Do qualified leads progress to calls and proposals?

Do not expand to additional niches until the ecommerce hypothesis has meaningful evidence.

## Future expansion

Once the ecommerce test produces reliable data, build separate focused landings/campaigns rather than turning one homepage into a catalog of services:

- `/uk/ai` → AI automation.
- `/uk/opencart` → OpenCart owners.
- `/uk/automation` → business automation.
- `/uk/keycrm` → CRM/integration demand.
- Other verticals only after evidence supports them.

- [ ] Confirm exact $350 package scope.
- [ ] Define ecommerce ICP and exclusions.
- [ ] Define conversion events and qualified-lead definition.
- [ ] Audit current analytics/GTM/Google Ads tracking.

### Phase 2: Landing
- [ ] Create `/uk/ecommerce`.
- [ ] Implement focused hero and CTA.
- [ ] Add deliverables and qualification section.
- [ ] Add ecommerce process.
- [ ] Add strongest relevant case/proof.
- [ ] Add pricing/package scope.
- [ ] Add FAQ and final CTA.
- [ ] Ensure mobile-first performance and accessibility.

### Phase 3: Ads
- [ ] Create focused Search campaign.
- [ ] Separate tightly related keyword groups.
- [ ] Write ads matching the landing offer.
- [ ] Add negative keywords.
- [ ] Start at 200 UAH/day.

### Phase 4: Measurement
- [ ] Verify events end-to-end.
- [ ] Monitor search terms and lead quality.
- [ ] Record objections and sales outcomes.
- [ ] Review performance on a fixed cadence.

### Phase 5: Optimization
- [ ] Run one major landing/offer test at a time.
- [ ] Improve qualification based on real conversations.
- [ ] Update proof and objections based on real leads.
- [ ] Decide whether to scale, reposition, or stop the hypothesis.

## Strategic principle

**One niche → one offer → one landing → one campaign → measurable feedback.**

The objective is to make Neuronix learn what converts before adding more services and audiences.
